🌭 SausageMath

Sausage-making math that holds up. Cure weighed to the gram, fat on target, casings counted - and nothing eyeballed that shouldn't be.

Cure #1, weighed not guessed

Prague powder #1 is 6.25% sodium nitrite. The target is parts per million of meat, and there is a hard ceiling - this is the one number you never eyeball.

Fat ratio

Venison and chicken breast come to sausage nearly naked. The fat you add back is the juiciness - 20-30% of the finished block.

Salt & casing

Salt runs 1.8-2% of the block and casing footage runs on feet per pound - both scale with the block you actually ground.

Yield honesty

The smoker and the drying chamber charge rent in water weight. Plan servings on what comes out, not what went in.
